## Review, start and inspect

1. Open **Markets source review** on Markets. Inspect the existing source panels
   and retained packets.
2. Select the eligible board histories and BLS, BEA and Fed work, then choose
   **Preview selected source reads**.
   Name each exact board listing and each public source. Show the original
   source and retrieval clocks, retained results and selected Load or Refresh.
3. Select work explicitly. A missing-only plan keeps accepted packets unless
   their Refresh action is selected. A valid empty packet counts as checked.
   An unresolved or unadmitted board identity cannot become a price request.
4. Choose **Start selected source reads** once. Run selected reads in their declared order
   through the existing models and request admission. The current board has
   three listings, so the scope is at most three price histories and the three
   existing public-source operations. This is a bound, not a new request budget.
5. Inspect each result in the existing company board, day view and source
   panels. Distinguish accepted data, checked-empty, retained previous data,
   failed or cancelled work, and work that was not attempted.
6. End the pass before starting an existing chosen filing or due-check review.
   Their own Load, exact selection, Research, Back and separate Save still apply.

A preview does not fetch, save, write a note, complete a check or change membership.
The workflow must not load a newer Watchlist or Follow-ups version over an
unresolved or authored draft.

## Stops, retention and lifetime

An admission refusal, cooldown, invalid response or source failure stops the
remaining queue and leaves an explicit partial result. Do not infer a reset time
or retry automatically. **Cancel source review** stops pending and unattempted
work. Closing the disclosure or leaving its model ends a preview or running pass.
Late results
cannot revive the old pass. A new pass needs a new explicit preview and Start.

Each existing source keeps its accepted-packet, replacement and cancellation
rules. A retained packet remains previous evidence with its original clocks.
Do not use a non-null old packet as proof that a new read succeeded. Source
publication time, scheduled time and retrieval time remain distinct. The selected plan keeps the actions, identities and evidence captured before
Start; live source rows show subsequent outcomes. The pass adds no general freshness threshold, full-news claim, released economic value,
company relevance or common market timestamp.

Session, relevant catalog, view or selection changes retire old actions. A
changed plan or intervening source read requires a new preview. Opening company
research cancels remaining pass work. Retained Markets research and Back keep
only the evidence permitted by their existing route contract. Leaving for
Watchlist or Discover keeps the current board-price clearing rule. Existing
calendar/feed retention stays source-specific. Returning, foregrounding or
cooldown expiry never starts another request.

## Acceptance

- Mixed missing and retained packets run only the selected work, once and in
  the declared order. Exact identities and all original clocks remain visible.
- Checked-empty is distinct from never checked and a failed replacement. An
  invalid replacement follows the individual model's retention contract.
- Failure, cooldown, Cancel and retirement prevent later dispatch. Late
  completions, duplicate Start, returning to earlier values and reentry cannot
  revive the old pass or retarget its listing.
- Research and Back preserve the allowed packets and authored drafts. Existing
  filing/check review and independent online Saves keep their current scopes.
- Verify model result and cancellation boundaries, workspace integration,
  responsive/focus behavior through permitted checks, and a complete synthetic
  native journey before client delivery. Browser work remains subject to the
  owner's current browser stop. Automated checks do not establish owner-record
  or physical-device acceptance.
