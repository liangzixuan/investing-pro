## Complete workflow

choose the close date. Load the confirmed saved valuation collection separately.
Preview all current holdings in their existing order, including holdings outside
Watchlist. Show each exact listing's saved assumptions or the reason they cannot
be used.

Select Evaluate to calculate the existing six authored scenarios at that action
time. Compare each available result with the holding's admitted raw close on the
chosen date. A newer observation must not replace that close. Missing or invalid
chosen-date evidence must remain unavailable. Current saved assumptions evaluated
now are not historical valuation results or a backtest.

Keep the complete original Portfolio reading, holding quantities, raw assumption
maps, source packets, collection version and independent clocks. Open current
Research for a holding through the existing navigation. Keep its exact configured
issuer identity when restoring and saving revised assumptions. Revise inputs, calculate
and author a holding question or assessment through the current workbench and
check controls. Back returns to the original passive reading.

Portfolio, valuation inputs and Follow-ups keep separate Save and fresh Load
actions. This workflow does not add a holding to Watchlist, save a resource,
refresh a source during Preview/Evaluate, create a check from a passive result,
or calculate an aggregate portfolio fair value.

## Ownership

The captured reading belongs to the original Portfolio, selected date, source
packets, catalog, session and confirmed saved-input collection. The current
close-review component retires its Price controller when Research opens; the
retained reading therefore needs an explicit workspace-owned detached report
lease. It must not depend on a retired component or fabricate a new Price state.

An explicitly owned Research visit pauses group actions and preserves the reading.
Its owned Back restores actions only while the original lease and collection
remain current. Ledger, date, source, collection, catalog, session, file activity
and foreign navigation changes retire the lease. Equal-value replacement cannot
revive it. Reviewing and staging an unsaved valuation draft does not replace the
confirmed collection. Keep the passive reading until a Save, Load or another
ownership change retires it. Dirty workbench and resource conflict/uncertain-save
handling retain their existing boundaries.
